03 — How it works (BFS)
What it does

The app models the floor as a grid and runs Breadth-First Search (BFS) starting from all exits at once. BFS expands outward one step at a time, so the instant it reaches your pin, it has found the exit that is genuinely closest by walking distance — not straight-line distance through walls.
